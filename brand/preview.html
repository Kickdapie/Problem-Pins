03 · Rules

What keeps Pip from turning into clip art

  1. The body is always brand orange. Status colour lives only in the badge, the same five colours as the map dots.
  2. No mouth, no arms, no legs. Expression comes from the eyes, the tilt and the hop.
  3. Resident side and print only. In the Organization view Pip appears as the logo and nowhere else.
  4. One Pip per screen, at 48px or larger. Below that, use the mark.
  5. Flat fills. No gradients, outlines or drop shadows beyond the ground ellipse.
  6. One small loop per pose. Blink, sway, hop or sigh. It is CSS inside the SVG, and it stops under reduced motion.
